Every printed Recursive Tarot deck comes with $10 in recursive.eco credits — for readings, courses and the assistant. Send a photo of your order and we add the credits to your account.

How to claim
  1. Take a photo of your order — the Game Crafter confirmation email or the box in your hands.
  2. Email it to pp@playfulprocess.com with the email you use (or will use) on recursive.eco.
  3. We check it by hand and reply when the credits are in. This takes a few days, not minutes.

One claim per order. The credits go to one recursive.eco account and are not cash — they are spent on the site. If you have not made an account yet, just say so and we will hold the credits for you.
