Tarocco Piemontese — the Piedmontese Tarot (Solesio, 1865)

Draw & reflectPlay it on recursive.ecoOpen in the card viewer

At a glance

The tarot of Piedmont, the pack most played in northern Italy today, for games such as Scarto, Mitigati and Chiamare il Re. It derives from the Tarot de Marseille and was made reversible for play: 78 cards, 22 trumps numbered 0 to 21, and four suits of batons, cups, swords and coins.

Where it comes from

Tarot was made in Pinerolo by 1505, and Francesco Piscina described the game at Mondovì in 1565. Around 1700 Piedmontese cardmakers went out of business and players imported Marseille packs from France; when making resumed around 1735 the cards copied the Marseille pattern, French captions included. Local changes through the 19th century ended in the double-ended cards of today.

What is its own

  • The Angel above the World: in most games trump 20 outranks 21, as in Bologna.
  • The Fool as excuse: it beats nothing but can always be played.
  • Two directions of pips: batons and swords rank ten down to one; cups and coins one up to ten .
  • It was the standard tarot of the Kingdom of Sardinia, so it was also played in Savoy and Nice .

In this library

All 78 cards of the pack F. F. Solesio published in Turin in 1865, from the copy at the Bibliothèque nationale de France.
