The 'Mantegna Tarocchi' — A Ladder of Being (NOT a Tarot), c. 1465

Play it on recursive.ecoOpen in the card viewer

At a glance

A set of fifty 15th-century engravings, not a card game and not a tarot deck at all. Included in this library precisely to explain the confusion: the name is a later error. Not by Mantegna, not tarot, not for play or divination — a didactic image cycle of the cosmos and the social order.

Origin & dating

Two sets survive, the E-series (c.1465, the older) and the S-series (a little later). Documentary support includes a Bolognese manuscript dated 1467. Stylistic links to the Salone dei Mesi at the Palazzo Schifanoia point to artists in the Ferrarese / Este orbit (around Francesco del Cossa); the true engraver is unknown. The attribution to Andrea Mantegna is spurious.

Provenance & evidence

Fifty engravings in five decades of ten: social ranks (Beggar → Pope), the Muses and Apollo, the liberal arts, the virtues and cosmic principles, and the celestial spheres. It is a ladder of being — a Renaissance encyclopedia in pictures.

Game or divination?

Neither. No suits, no trick-taking structure, no fortune-telling. Best understood as educational prints, plausibly for instructing upper-class youths — though, honestly, no document records their actual use. The "Tarocchi" label was pinned on later by writers who confused them with the picture-rich tarot game.

The fear question

Not applicable — these are humanist study images, openly produced and circulated. Their inclusion here is corrective: to stop the genealogy absorbing material that merely looks tarot-adjacent.

Counter-voices

Esoteric authors have repeatedly raided the Mantegna series for "hidden" Neoplatonic or Hermetic keys to tarot. The series is genuinely Neoplatonic — that was mainstream learned culture — but it is not an ancestor of the tarot pack. Treating it as one is how false genealogies (and the Egyptian story) get built.

Open questions / corrections owed

  • Function (teaching aid? collector's cycle? devotional ladder?) is inferred, not documented.
  • Relationship, if any, between its iconographic program and early trionfi trumps is a matter of influence, not descent.
