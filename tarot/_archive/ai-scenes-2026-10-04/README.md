# Archived: the AI-written "Scene" sections (removed October 4 2026)

**What they were.** Every historical deck carried a "Scene" section on each card, shown as
"Editorial — The Recursive Tarot · what is pictured on this card". They were written by Claude
sessions in 2026, by looking at the card images with a vision model (the Sola Busca minors on
June 13 2026, for example, were described by downloading the images and reading them one by one),
and sometimes from memory of a card.

**Why they were removed.** PlayfulProcess read them on her phone and found them unreliable as a
record of the cards. The Golden Dawn deck's Sun, for instance, described Waite-Smith's picture
(a child on a horse, sunflowers above a wall) under a "Book T" label, while the Golden Dawn's own
Sun follows its own description. A machine description drifts toward the best-known version of a
card and can add details the picture does not have. Her rule since that day: **where a tradition
wrote its cards down, the card carries that tradition's own text** (Book T, Waite's *Pictorial
Key*, Etteilla); new meaning is written only for decks no tradition fully held, and it is labelled
as ours.

**What is here.** One file per deck, `<slug>.json`: the deck name, how many Scenes were removed,
and each removed Scene by item id and card name. 1,500 sections from 22 grammars. Nothing else
was changed in those cards.

**To revisit.** Treat them as machine drafts, never as a source. If one is ever used again, it is
checked against the card image and the tradition's text by a person, and labelled "machine
description, checked by …".

**Note for recursive.eco.** The app's copies of these grammars still carry their Scenes, and on a
write-back ("Resolve all drifts") the app's items win. Remove them from the app copies too, or
the next write-back brings them back here.
