# Yve Lepkowski's licence: where it is stated

Checked **2026-10-05** by reading every page and post of stolen-thyme.com through its WordPress
API (311 items), after an Oct 4 pass that looked only at the home, about, deck and guidebook pages
and missed it.

- **Where:** <https://stolen-thyme.com/downloads/>, section "Terms of Use". It says the work on that
  page is "licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License",
  with a link to the licence.
- **What the page covers:** the free downloads of Tarocchino Arlecchino, Arlecchino's Augmented
  Arcana, Anecdotes Tarot (pocket edition), Petit Lenormand, Clown Town Tarot and Clown Town Playing
  Cards, including the guidebook files. Each download also carries an `Information.txt` with source
  and licence information.
- **Her terms, summarised:** credit her as the creator and link to her website; state the licence
  (CC BY-SA 4.0) and link it; say what was modified, including earlier modifications; share
  adaptations under the same licence; add no further legal or technical restrictions.
- **How this repo meets them:** each of her five grammars carries `_grammar_commons.attribution`
  (her name, role, link), `license` + `license_url`, and `modifications`.
- **What her licence cannot cover:** the Anecdotes Tarot and Petit Lenormand are inspired by Joanna
  Newsom's songs. Lyrics belong to their songwriter and publisher, not to Yve, so they are not
  stored here (removed Oct 4 2026; see `docs/rights/RIGHTS-AUDIT-2026-10-04.md`).
