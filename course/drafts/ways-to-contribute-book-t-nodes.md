# Node: Contribute to Book T

**id:** `lesson-04b-contribute-to-book-t` · **category:** lesson

**Content:**

Book T is the Golden Dawn's own book of the tarot, printed in 1912 and in the public domain. The [Book T deck](https://recursive.eco/g/2000ad96-70c6-4aa4-a6fb-c22cce5e9cc7) keeps its words and order, and its pictures are a first set, meant to be improved. Other hands are welcome.

You can add a picture for one card, a whole style (a Major Arcana in linocut, one suit in watercolour), a correction where a picture or the transcription misses Book T's words, or a translation of a card's passage.

Book T's words decide every symbol: quote the passage you drew from. Hand-made and AI-made are both welcome; say which. Credit yourself. Contributions are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

I review every contribution before it appears in the deck. The [Book T: a Community Deck](course-viewer.html?course=book-t-community-deck) lesson has the rules and the steps.

# Node: Send a picture for a card

**id:** `lesson-04c-send-a-picture` · **category:** lesson

**Content:**

The way that works today is a pull request on GitHub. You need a free account, nothing else.

1. Find the card's id in [the deck's file](https://github.com/PlayfulProcess/recursive-tarot/blob/main/tarot/book-t/grammar.json), for example `cups-ace`.
2. Name your picture `<your-style>--<card-id>.jpg`: portrait, about 2 by 3, under 1 MB, no lettering in it.
3. In the [plates folder](https://github.com/PlayfulProcess/recursive-tarot/tree/main/tarot/book-t/plates), choose **Add file → Upload files**, then **Propose changes**.
4. In the pull request, write the card, the Book T words you drew from, your style's name, whether it's hand-made or AI-made, and the name to credit.

When I accept it, your picture shows under the card's own picture, with your name.
