Card Table

Lay cards across the areas of your life (rows) and what to do with each: less · keep · more (columns). Then sit with the table, notice where the major arcana fall, circle the cards that carry the message, and decide for yourself. The cards ask; you decide. How the practice works →

Reading the codes

  • Major arcana are Roman numerals, 0 (the Fool) to XXI (the World). They have a gold frame on the table.
  • Minor arcana are rank + suit: A or 1, 2–10, P page, Kn knight, Q queen, K king; then W wands, C cups, S swords, P pentacles. So 4C is the Four of Cups, KnP the Knight of Pentacles.
  • ↓ after a card means it fell reversed (r works too); ↑ means upright and can be left out.
  • ( ) around a card means it is circled: the card that carries the message, the one that feels needed, not the "best" one. It has a ring on the table.
  • Two cards in one cell are separated by a space: 8C 3P.

Untick show codes once you know them. The codes are also how an assistant can read your table.

Write it by hand (the notation)

    One line per area: Area | cards | cards | cards. A line in [brackets] is a heading. > … is a note. Label: cards is a mini-row under the table (another deck or oracle). deck: and columns: lines set the deck and the column names.
