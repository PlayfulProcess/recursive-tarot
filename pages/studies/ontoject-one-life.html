The Ontoject, one life

The trumps of the Ontoject deck read as the development of one being, from before conception to return. One possible reading, made knowing it is made. The words are in the study.

Speed

Three cards

The zero, the middle, the end. Each loops slowly, in under twenty seconds at 1×; Speed slows every animation on the page, and with motion off they hold their key pose.

The sequence

All 22, as simple glyphs morphing one into the next, so the arc can be felt. In play, the Trickster steps in between every card: it copies the card, a little too much, and slips into the next. Turn on Reversed to see every card overdone.
