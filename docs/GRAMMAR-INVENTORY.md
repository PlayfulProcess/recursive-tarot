# Grammar inventory

Every `tarot/<slug>/grammar.json` in this repo: what it is, how many items it carries, which
viewers render it, whether the recursive.eco app syncs it, and what this audit found wrong.

Generated by hand from a full pass on **2026-07-27**. Counts are item counts (`items[]`),
including non-card items (suits, emergences, keyword nodes, axes).

**61 grammars total** — 36 decks/collections in `tarot/_collection.json`, 23 courses,
plus the meta grammar and one unlisted deck.

---

## How to read the columns

- **App-synced** — the slug has an entry in `tarot/_eco_ids.json`, i.e. recursive.eco holds a
  copy and syncs it from **`main`**. Everything else is **site-only**: it renders on
  tarot.recursive.eco but the app doesn't know about it. (Reminder: the live site is served
  from `claude/website-content-cards-plan-xwn8lj`, not `main` — see CLAUDE.md.)
- **Embeds into it** — items in this grammar that carry the `source_deck`/`source_item_id`
  cross-link, so another grammar's content is resolved into their detail view. Since Jul 27
  2026 that embed is always **framed** (`RefResolve.renderEmbed`) — see "Finding 1" below.

## Which viewers render what

Almost every viewer is generic and takes `?src=../tarot/<slug>/grammar.json`, so any grammar
can open in any of them. What differs is the **default** and what the site actually links to:

| Viewer | Default source | Renders |
|---|---|---|

| `deck.html` (repo root) | `?file=` required | Flat static deck page — every item, every section, no JS filtering. Uses `RT.md` from `loader.js`. |

## What this audit found

### Finding 1 — cross-grammar embeds rendered unframed *(fixed)*

**What was wrong.** An item carrying `metadata.source_deck` + `metadata.source_item_id` gets
the *other* grammar's content resolved into its detail view. That content used to render in
the host's own section markup, so the two grammars were visually indistinguishable.

Two directions, both live on the site:

- **person → card.** Opening Pamela Colman Smith in `people-of-tarot` appended the Golden Dawn
  Two of Wands' `Scene / Symbol / Divinatory Meaning / Golden Dawn Title / Correspondences`
  onto her biography, as though they were part of it.
- **card → person** — *the much bigger one, ~1,000 items.* Every one of the 78 Golden Dawn
  cards appended her entire biography (`Who / Claim vs the record / In this collection`), and
  the same pattern runs through **11 decks**: 120 Visconti-Sforza items, 118 Marseille, 108
  Sola Busca, 105 Cary-Yale, 104 Golden Dawn, 94 × 3 Etteilla, 83 Viéville, 64 Mantegna, and
  more. A card's page ended in somebody's life story.

**The fix.** All framing now lives in one place — `viewers/reference-resolve.js`
(`RefResolve.renderEmbed`), used by `viewers/cards.html`, `viewers/tree-viewer.html` and
`viewers/caster-studio.html`. The embed is a bounded, **collapsed** `<details>` box that names
the relationship and says whose content it is:

| Case | Summary reads | Default |
|---|---|---|
| person/institution host, card embed | "Featured card from Golden Dawn Tarot — 'Two of Wands'" | collapsed |
| card host, person embed | "About Pamela Colman Smith ('Pixie')" | collapsed |
| card host, book embed | "Source text: <book>" | collapsed |
| card host, card in another deck | "The same card in <deck>" | collapsed |
| **aggregator host** (`all-decks-many-lenses`) | "Full entry from <deck>" | **expanded** |

The aggregator is the one exception: its items are pointer stubs whose own text literally says
"Open the source deck for this card's full interpretation", so there the embed *is* the content.

### Finding 2 — Book T: two cards had swallowed the rest of Waite's book *(fixed)*

The 104-item grammar was **structurally complete** — 78 cards, all with `image_url` and
sections, 22 majors + 56 minors, 26 non-card items (6 emergences, 19 keyword nodes, 1 axis)
that all render. The Section Lens hides nothing here (nothing in this deck uses the bracketed
`[Author, Work, Year]` convention that triggers auto-hiding, since the Jul 27 restructure
replaced those with italic editorial headers).

What was actually broken was **transcription overflow**:

| Item | `Waite` section was | Should be |
|---|---:|---|
| `pentacles-ace` | **59,291 chars** | 488 |
| `major-21-the-world` | **19,875 chars** | 1,401 |

Every other card's `Waite` section is 396–2,072 characters. Those two had absorbed the tail of
A. E. Waite's *Pictorial Key* — Part II §2.3, Part III §3.1, and §§3.3–3.9 (the
divinatory-meanings appendix, the Celtic Cross, the alternative reading methods, the 35-card
method) — plus, on the World, a **verbatim repeat of 16 court/pip card texts that are already
correct on their own cards**. Opening the Ace of Pentacles rendered ~60,000 characters of book.

Fixed by truncating each section at the card's own text. **Nothing was deleted**: all 78,505
removed characters are preserved in `research/sources/waite-pictorial-key-part-iii.md` with
provenance headers. `scripts/normalize_book_t_sections.py --check` reports 0 changed and
"no-content-lost: OK"; `scripts/check_all.py` passes.

**Still open, for the builder:**

- `major-20-judgement` is the **only major** without a `Papus` section (21 of 22 have one).
  Filling it needs the actual Papus text — not something to invent.
- The 36 numbered pips (2–10 in each suit) have no `Research note`; the 22 majors, 16 courts
  and 4 aces do. That's the visible asymmetry between clicking a pip and clicking a court card.
  It's a coverage gap in `research/cards/golden-dawn-book-t-tarot.md`, not a bug.
- **OCR noise** in the `Papus` sections of the occult decks. `major-01-the-magician` (Book T),
  `arcanum-01-le-bateleur` (Oswald Wirth) and `atout-01` (Court de Gébelin) each carry ~9,4 KB
  where the median is ~3 KB, and all three open with scan garbage like
  `; le-batelfijr` / `^** I ' ■■-■ ■■ * ^.J`, then run past the end of their own entry into the
  next card's heading. Same class of defect as Finding 2 but smaller; needs a careful pass.

### Finding 3 — links weren't clickable *(fixed)*

677 markdown links live in section text across the grammars. They rendered as literal
`[label](https://…)` in the static viewers.

- **`deck.html`** was the worst: `RT.md` in `loader.js` handled only bold/italic. The Book T
  deck page alone showed **42 literal markdown links and 0 anchors**, and rendered `#`/`##`
  headings as literal hashes (which is much of why long deck descriptions looked broken).
  `RT.md` now does links, bare URLs, `code`, and headings. → 42 anchors, 0 literal links.
- **`viewers/cards.html`** and **`viewers/tree-viewer.html`** already handled `[text](url)`;
  they now also linkify **bare URLs**.
- **`viewers/caster-studio.html`** rendered card sections through `esc()` alone — no links at
  all. It now has an `mdLite` helper used for both the drawn card's own sections and the embed.
- **`viewers/grammar-course.html`** gained bare-URL linkification.
- **`viewers/explorer.html`** deliberately left alone: its "Sections" mode is a dense tile
  that is itself a click target (opens the full card), so anchors inside it would fight the
  tile's click handler.

All anchors get `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Trailing sentence punctuation is
pushed outside the `href`.

**Retired-branch links.** 504 links pointed at `blob/dev/` — a branch that is stale *and*
divergent. All rewritten to `blob/main/` (486 in 23 grammars, plus `course/history-of-tarot.mdx`,
`course/how-to-contribute.mdx`, `pages/games/{tarocchino,trionfi}.html`, `print/book/book.html`,
and one archived script). All 33 unique target paths exist on `main`; sampled URLs return
HTTP 200. The two `course/*.mdx` files had to be fixed too, or `course_to_grammar.py` would
reintroduce the dev links on the next regeneration.

### Finding 4 — scripts archived

27 one-shot scripts moved to `scripts/archive/` with a README explaining that the grammar JSONs
are canonical and these must not be re-run. 24 live scripts stayed. Nothing deleted, nothing
renamed, no import broken — verified that none of the moved scripts is imported by, or
subprocessed from, anything that stayed. The `tgc_card.py` + `build_sampler.py` +
`prebake_deck_r2.py` + `build_sampler_v6.py` + `detect_flood_bg.py` cluster imports each other
by bare module name and stayed together.

## Other things noticed (not changed)

Language and content notes, for the separate editorial pass — **flagged only, nothing rewritten.**

- **`marsha-linehan-reads-the-tarot-course` names a living person.** CLAUDE.md's rule is
  explicit: *"Name a school, not a living person — voices drawn from a living teacher are
  titled by their tradition… only dead, eponymous figures (Kant, Jung) carry their own name."*
  Every sibling course follows it — `hospicing-modernity-…`, `non-dual-tantra-…`,
  `post-activism-…`. This one doesn't. Renaming it to the tradition (DBT) would touch the
  slug, `course/*.mdx`, `voices.json` and any inbound links, so it needs a deliberate decision.
- **Nested emphasis renders as literal asterisks.** `research/people/pamela-colman-smith.md`
  writes `**Rider-Waite-*Smith***`; the viewers' bold rule is `\*\*([^*]+)\*\*`, which can't
  match across the inner `*`, so the live page shows `**Rider-Waite-Smith**` with the asterisks
  visible. Cheapest fix is in the dossier (drop the inner emphasis), then rebuild people.
- **Citation keys render raw.** `[@web_pcs_wp; @web_rws_wopc]` appears verbatim in the rendered
  text across the people and card grammars. There's no bibliography resolver in the static
  viewers, so every sourced claim ends in visible key soup. Either resolve them or hide them.
- **`_collection.json` blurbs are just the description's first line**, so Book T's reads
  `# Golden Dawn (Book T) — full chapter` — a markdown heading, leaked into a summary field.
- **`christmas-the-rebirth`** is the only collection deck with no app id, and it's
  `is_published: false`. Intentional?
- **Stale script names in the docs.** `docs/plan/DEV-BRANCH-PLANS.md` names
  `validate_grammars.py`, `docs/plan/REARCHITECT-GENEALOGY.md` names `build_catalog.py`,
  `print/book/README.md` names `validate-grammar.py` — none of these exist. Pre-dates this
  audit; `docs/plan/*` is historical planning material and was left alone.
