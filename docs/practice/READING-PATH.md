# A reading path

One short reading a day. Each day is one section of a course and one card to sit with.
Read the section, open the card, look at it for a minute before you read anything about it.
Then, if you like, write one line about what you saw.

There are 177 days here. Skip days, repeat days, stop for a week. The path waits.

A few things to know:

- The order runs from why to read at all, through your own practice and the voices, into the
  history, and ends with making. You can start any part on its own.
- The card is chosen to sit beside the section: sometimes the section names it, sometimes it is
  a card from a deck the section talks about, sometimes it is simply a deck you have not visited
  for a while. The path visits 35 of the 36 decks in the library (Christmas: The
  Rebirth has no card images yet).
- When you finish a section in practice, mark it in the
  [revision ledger](REVISION-LEDGER.md).
- Courses left out of the daily path, because they are about tools rather than practice:
  [Ways to Contribute](https://tarot.recursive.eco/pages/course-viewer.html?course=how-to-contribute) and
  [Working with Claude Desktop](https://tarot.recursive.eco/pages/course-viewer.html?course=working-with-claude-desktop).
  [Reading the Cards](https://tarot.recursive.eco/pages/course-viewer.html?course=reading-the-cards) is the anthology of the voice courses,
  so its parts are already here, one course at a time.
