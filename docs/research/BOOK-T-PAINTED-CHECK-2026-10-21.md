# Book T, painted with its symbols: does each painting fit the 1912 text?

October 21 2026. PlayfulProcess: "can you have an agent looking at the images and text and see if they are compatible?"

**What was checked.** All 78 cards of `tarot/book-t/painted-symbols/` (the official Book T pictures since Oct 21 2026), rendered to PNG by the script's own renderer (`python scripts/build_book_t_painted_symbols.py --only <ids> --png DIR`), each looked at against its card's `Book T (1912)` section in `tarot/book-t/grammar.json` (what the picture should show: hands, clouds, number and arrangement of the suit emblems, roses, lotuses, flames, figures, colours) and against the band's symbols (planet, sign, letter, element, decan, Path). Four reviewers, one per group, counting from zoomed crops. This is a machine reading of the pictures, unchecked by a person: a count here is a candidate for her eye, not a verdict.

**Verdicts.** ok = the painting shows what the text says; minor = a small detail missing or loose; mismatch = the text is contradicted (a wrong count of the suit emblem, a wrong figure), which only a new painting fixes.

**The Keys.** Book T's 1912 text for the 22 Keys gives titles, attributions and meanings, not a description of the picture, so the Keys were checked only on what the text states (title, attribution, Hebrew letter, Path). All 22 bands are right.

## The worst five

1. **Wands 6 to 10**: the painter draws 2+2 crossed wands where Book T has 3+3 or 4+4. wands-06 has 4 crossed + 2 short (six crossed three and three wanted); wands-07 has 5 of 7; wands-08 has 4 crossed + 4 short (eight crossed four and four wanted); wands-09 has 5 of 9; wands-10 has 6 of 10. In 7 to 10 the hands are not clasped in pairs.
2. **pentacles-10**: 14 pentacles where Book T has 10, and treasure chests the text does not name.
3. **pentacles-08**: 11 pentacles where Book T has 8 in the figure Populus (two columns of four), and 5 roses where it has 4.
4. **wands-ace**: the club is painted upside down (its three flaming branches hang below the fist) and has 12 Yod flames where Book T has 22.
5. **cups-03**: 4 cups where Book T has 3 in an erect triangle, and the hand holds the top cup instead of the lotuses.

## Re-paint list (do not fix by data: the painting itself is wrong)

Fourteen cards. Each line says what the new painting must show, from Book T's words. No images were generated.

- **wands-ace**: the club is upside down (branches hang below the fist) and there are 12 Yods instead of 22. New painting: a white radiating hand issuing from clouds grasps a heavy club with its three branches rising UP; the left and right branches end in 3 flames each, the centre in 4 (10 in all); the branches bear the scale sigils; 22 small Yod flames surround it: 3 below the right branch, 7 above the centre branch, and 12 about the left branch (6 above, 6 below). The whole reads as one great flaming torch.
- **pentacles-knight**: the sceptre carries a 7-8 pointed star, not a hexagram, and the pentacle is a plain pentagram coin. New painting: keep the rider, horse, antelope crest and corn, but make the sceptre's head a clear hexagram (two interlaced triangles) and the pentacle the Zelator Adeptus Minor Earth pentacle (a disc divided into segments with a hexagram), not a pentagram. If only one part can be redone, the hexagram matters most.
- Optional, borderline (rated minor): **swords-ace**, 8 drops falling from the crown where the text gives six Vaus falling from the sword's point.
- **wands-06** — only 4 crossed wands. Must show two white hands clasped in the centre (First Order grip) holding six wands crossed three and three (3 leaning each way), flames leaping from the junction, plus one short flaming wand above and one below.
- **wands-07** — 5 wands, upper hands unclasped. Must show two hands clasped in the grip holding six wands (three crossed each way), and a third hand from a cloud at the bottom holding one upright wand passing between them (7 wands in all), flames at the junction, short wand above and below.
- **pentacles-08** — 11 pentacles, 5 roses. Must show 8 pentacles in two columns of four (Populus), a white radiant hand from a cloud grasping a rose branch with four white roses touching only the four lowest pentacles and only leaves (no buds) touching the four upper ones; pentacles like the Ace without cross and wings.
- **pentacles-09** — 10 pentacles, 7 roses. Must show 9 pentacles in the nine-dot figure (Book T figure: 1 top, then pairs, so total 9), a white radiant hand holding a rose branch with nine white roses, each touching one pentacle, with rosebuds on the branches too.
- **pentacles-10** — 14 pentacles plus chests. Must show exactly 10 pentacles in rows of 2, 1, 2, 2, 1, 2, an angelic hand holding the branch at its lower end, roses touching every pentacle, no buds; drop the treasure chests.
- **wands-08** — 4 crossed wands. Must show four white radiant hands from clouds, two from each side, clasped in two pairs in the centre (First Order grip), holding eight wands crossed four with four, flames at the junction, one short flaming wand at top centre and one at bottom centre.
- **wands-09** — 5 wands. Must show the wands-08 composition (four hands clasped in pairs, eight wands crossed four and four) plus a fifth hand at the foot holding one upright wand through the junction, flames leaping there (9 wands).
- **wands-10** — 6 wands, six side hands. Must show four hands (two clasped pairs) holding eight wands crossed four and four, and a fifth hand at the foot holding two upright wands through the junction, flames issuing (10 wands).
- **pentacles-03** — 4 pentacles, hand on a disc. Must show 3 pentacles in an equilateral triangle (one at the apex, two below), a white-winged angelic hand holding a rose branch whose two white rosebuds touch and rise above the topmost pentacle.
- **wands-04** — Wrong: only two wands cross in the centre. New painting: two white radiating hands issuing from clouds right and left, clasped in the centre (grip of the First Order), holding FOUR wands (torches) crossed, flames issuing from the point of junction; two small flaming wands above and below.
- **pentacles-07** — Wrong: pentacles laid out 2/3/1/1 instead of the geomantic figure Rubeus; seven buds instead of five; buds not over the uppermost pentacles. New painting: white radiating hand from a cloud holding a white rose branch; seven pentacles in the Rubeus pattern (rows of 2, 1, 2, 2 from the top); exactly five rosebuds overhanging, but not touching, the five uppermost pentacles.
- **cups-03** — Wrong: four cups; the hand holds the top cup instead of the lotuses. New painting: white radiating hand holding a group of lotuses; THREE cups in an erect equilateral triangle (one at top, two below); two flowers rise either side of and overhang the top cup, pouring white water into it, and flowers likewise pour into the lower cups; all three overflow, the top one into the two others, and these onto the lower part of the card.

(Optional, minor, if repainting anyway: wands-02 small wands should bear five flames each and the hand should issue from clouds with rays; pentacles-05 should show the branch bare with every rose falling; swords-07 rose should be held by the central hand.)

## Band (symbol) notes

- **wands-10**: the top-left letter is Vav (ו), where every other Wands card has Yod (י). The band follows the card's text, which in our transcription reads "Malkuth of HB:V" (`research/sources/book-t-equinox-1912.txt`, line 1856). Wands are Yod throughout, so this is either a misprint in the 1912 issue or a transcription slip. Check the scan page; if 1912 prints Yod, correct the transcription; if 1912 prints Vav, keep it and note it as Book T's own misprint (the band could then show Yod with a note).
- **pentacles-06**: Taurus is on top and the Moon below. This is by design: the script puts on top whichever Book T names first, and Book T writes "Taurus and Moon". Not an error.
- **key-19-sun** (minor): a stray painted monogram in the painting's lower right corner; a retouch or crop, not a re-paint.
- **key-15-devil**: fits the text; both chained figures are painted fully nude. Worth a look before print.
- Every other planet, sign, letter, element, court span and Path agrees with the card's metadata.
